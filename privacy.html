Privacy: the anonymous data

OpenThinRDP is free, and anonymous data is how we learn which computers it runs on, how it is set up and used, how well it runs and what goes wrong, so we know what to fix and what to support first. You choose how much is sent, and this page lists all of it, item by item. Nothing on it can tell who you are, where you are or what you do in a session.

Three levels

Level What is sent
Full (the default) Once a week what the device is, how it is set up and used, how well it runs, and what went wrong
Errors only Only in a week when something went wrong: what went wrong, on which version and computer
Off Nothing

A new device asks at its first start, with Full ready and the three levels explained on the screen; nothing leaves it before the answer. Later: Anonymous data for the project, the last setting of the device, or ANON_STATS (full, errors, off) for a whole site or group from the manager. The manager has the same three levels for itself (Settings > Manager). Devices and managers that sent anonymous counts before 0.3.3 and manager 1.1.7 send Full.

sudo /opt/openthinrdp/stats.sh --print on a device prints exactly what it would send now, and sends nothing.

What the manager sends

Its first start shows, once, what the manager and the devices send and where each is set. Half a minute after it starts the first time, then once a day while it is used.

The day (manager_day, Full only):

What Example
Manager version, Windows, language of the window 1.1.7, Windows Server 2025 Datacenter, it
How many devices it manages, in ranges 1-5, 6-20, 21-100, more than 100
The size of what it reads, as counts: hosts, VMs, clusters, domain controllers, servers, brokers, SQL replicas, iDRAC, Proxmox, vCenter, Veeam, Citrix, custom checks 2, 22, 1, 2
The pages opened and the actions used since the last send, by their fixed names, with how many times health: 3, vm.start: 1

What went wrong (manager_errors, Full and Errors only, only on a day with errors): version, Windows, language and devices as above, and how many errors the manager and its window wrote in their logs. Never what they say.

Feedback (manager_feedback): once a week, until it is sent, the manager asks how it is going (also from Settings > Manager > Send feedback). Only when you press Send, and only these: the vote (1 to 5) and what it is used for, if chosen (home, office, trying). The words you write are not sent by the manager: it opens a discussion on GitHub already written, in your browser, and you decide whether to post it (with your GitHub account, in public). Later asks again in a week. Nothing when the anonymous data are off.

What is never sent

The name of a device or of a computer, IP or MAC addresses, user names, passwords, the domain, the names or addresses of servers and brokers, settings, the text of logs and error messages, anything typed on a device, anything shown in a session.

Where it goes

To PostHog in its European cloud (Frankfurt). The project is set to keep no IP address; each send carries a random identifier made for it alone, kept nowhere and taken from nothing of the device or of you, no person profile and no place from the address. The project sees totals and averages, such as HP t530: 12 devices, sign-in 4.2 s, never a device followed over time. Devices before 0.3.3 and managers before 1.1.7 sent fewer counts to GoatCounter, also without any IP address.

What stays at your site

Not sent anywhere, but worth knowing: to show the experience (0.2.23), each device writes in its event log how long each sign-in took (with the user name) and the latency and bandwidth of each session, and the manager keeps these 40 days in health\experience\ of the management folder. They are technical data of the service, readable by whoever reads that folder; in some countries measuring the work of employees has rules of its own (in Italy art. 4 of the Statuto dei lavoratori): tell the users, as for the other logs of the fleet.

Choosing or switching it off

  • One device: Anonymous data for the project, the last setting of the device: Full, Errors only or Off.
  • Every device of a site: the same setting in the manager, last in the Settings of the site (or of a group), or ANON_STATS=off (or errors) in common.conf.
  • Before the first start: ANON_STATS=off in image/site/site.conf of your own image: the question is not asked and nothing is ever sent. ANON_STATS in common.conf holds from the moment a device has taken the central settings: a new device sends its first data, with the level chosen on its screen, before that.
  • The manager: Settings > Manager > Anonymous data for the project.

When it is off, nothing is sent. A device with no internet sends nothing and tries again at its next report.
